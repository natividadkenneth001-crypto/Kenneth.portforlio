AI-Powered Daily Automation

🎯 The Problem

Business owners waste time manually thinking of daily social media posts or team motivation messages, which can be easily automated with AI.

💡 The Solution

I built an n8n workflow that triggers every morning, uses Groq AI to generate a unique quote, and automatically posts it to a Discord channel via Webhook.

⚙️ How It Works:

  • ✓ Schedule Trigger runs at 8:00 AM
  • ✓ Groq AI generates the text
  • ✓ Webhook sends the data to Discord

🛠 Tech Stack: n8n, Groq AI, Discord Webhooks, JSON

✅ Status: Fully Automated & Working

📈 The Result

Saves 15-30 minutes of manual work daily. The system runs flawlessly in the background, delivering fresh content without human intervention.
